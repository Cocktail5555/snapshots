What it does
01

Shoot

Controls drawn like hardware: a big shutter, a film dial with five styles, a zoom step for every lens, flash and self-timer.

02

Print

Every photo feeds out of a printer with the sound of the motor, and develops as you watch. Shake to hurry it.

03

Booth

Press once for three poses on a countdown, printed together as one tall strip.

04

Keep

A caption on the front in six fonts, a private memory on the back, a gallery, widgets and a nightstand clock.

iPhone Duo

Built around the fold.

The layout changes with every pose, using both displays and the hinge.

Seated
Half open: viewfinder on the upright half, controls on the flat half. Take the shot, unfold it flat, and the print feeds out across the whole display.
Landscape
The viewfinder on one screen, a full camera body on the other, with a switch to swap sides.
Portrait
A large viewfinder above the controls, and a print that fills the screen.
Closed
A pocket camera on the front display, with the self-timer and Booth a tap away.
Standing
Stood up like a tent: a clock with your snapshots beside it.
The maker

Ryan waited in line for the original iPhone and has wanted to build an app of his own ever since. iPhone Duo was the reason to finally start. Snapshots is his first app, made to feel the way that first iPhone did: simple, surprising and fun.
