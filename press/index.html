What it does

A camera with a printer built in. Every photo feeds out of a printer at the top of the screen as a framed instant print, with the sound of the motor, and develops as you watch. Shake the phone and it develops immediately.

Controls designed like hardware. A large shutter, a film dial that clicks through five styles (Classic, Gold, Coast, Sepia, Mono), a zoom step for each lens, flash, self-timer and front camera. Light and dark themes.

Captions and a gallery. Write a handwritten-style caption along the bottom of a print, then keep it in the gallery, share it, or save it to Photos with its border and caption.

A nightstand clock. Turned on its side while charging, the app becomes a clock with your prints shuffling past beside it.

Designed for iPhone Duo

Seated: stand the Duo half open, with the viewfinder on the upright half and the controls on the flat half. Take the shot and the print waits in the printer. Unfold the Duo flat and the print feeds out across the whole display.

Landscape: the viewfinder on one screen and a full camera body on the other, with a switch to swap sides.

Portrait: a large viewfinder above the controls, and a print that fills the screen.

Closed: a pocket camera on the front display.

Standing: stood up like a tent, a clock with your snapshots beside it.

iPhone Duo screens were captured in Apple's iPhone Duo simulator.

About the maker

Ryan Fredericks waited in line for the original iPhone and has wanted to build an app of his own ever since. iPhone Duo was the reason to finally start. Snapshots is his first app, made to feel the way that first iPhone did: simple, surprising and fun.
