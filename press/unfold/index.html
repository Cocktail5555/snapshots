New feature

Booth Mode

A photo booth in your pocket. Set the timer to Booth, press the shutter once, and strike three poses on a countdown. All three print together as one tall snapshot.

  • One press, three photos. A count of three before each, so everyone is ready.
  • Made for selfies. Booth flips to the front camera for you.

Caption fonts

Six ways to write on your snapshot, from handwriting to typewriter. Pick one as you write your caption.

A memory on the back

Turn a snapshot over and write as much as you like about the moment. It stays on the back, just for you.
