Booth Mode

Three poses, one strip.

Set the timer to Booth, press the shutter once, and strike three poses on a countdown. All three print together as one tall snapshot.

  • One press, three photos. A count of three before each, so everyone is ready.
  • Made for selfies. Booth flips to the front camera for you.
  • A flash countdown, new in 1.2. On the back camera, the flash blinks through each count, so you know when to pose.
  • Hands-free on iPhone Duo. Stand it half open, step back, and unfold to print.
New in 1.2

Pin it to a board, or stick on the fridge.

Make a board for your prints: a corkboard, or a whole fridge to decorate. Move them around until it looks just right, then save it or send it to someone.

  • A fridge or a corkboard. A retro fridge in seven colors, modern stainless or black steel, or a classic corkboard.
  • Drag, pinch and twist. Move your prints, resize them and turn them, one finger or two.
  • Stickers, tape and notes. Add stickers, emojis, tape and handwritten notes, plus your own stickers from Messages: photo stickers, Memoji and Genmoji.
  • Save or share. Keep the finished board in Photos or send it to a friend.
New · iPhone Duo

Your prints beside the board

Open your iPhone Duo on its side and your prints sit on one screen, the fridge on the other. Drag your prints from the gallery directly onto your board.

Widgets

Your prints on your Home Screen.

Add a Snapshots widget and your favorite prints are there every time you unlock your phone, with a different one on top every hour.

  • Prints. One, two or three of your snapshots, in small, medium or large.

Designed like hardware

Controls you can almost feel.

Snapshots has a camera body, with parts that move the way real ones do.

  • A film dial that clicks round through five styles, always clockwise.
  • A zoom step for every lens your iPhone has. Slide along them for anything in between.
  • Flash, self-timer and the front camera, each one tap away.
  • Two themes, light and dark, or let it match your phone.

Meet the maker

Hi, I'm Ryan.

I've always been a huge fan of Apple products. I waited in line for the original iPhone, and I still remember the feeling of holding it for the first time: new, exciting and fun, like nothing I'd held before. It felt like the future!

Ever since that day I've wanted to build an app of my own. I just never found a reason big enough to start. iPhone Duo is that reason.

It brings that same feeling back, and inspired me to create something for it straight away. Snapshots is my first app, and the first thing I've ever felt compelled to make and share with the world.

I hope Snapshots gives you a little of that feeling too, and a shoebox full of memories worth keeping.

Ryan Fredericks
