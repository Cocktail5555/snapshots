Designed like hardware

Controls you can almost feel.

Snapshots has a camera body, with parts that move the way real ones do.

  • A film dial that clicks round through five styles, always clockwise.
  • A zoom step for every lens your iPhone has. Slide along them for anything in between.
  • Flash, self-timer and the front camera, each one tap away.
  • Two themes, light and dark, or let it match your phone.

Designed for the hardware

Controls you can almost feel.

Snapshots has a camera body, with parts that move the way real ones do.

  • A film dial that clicks round through five styles, always clockwise.
  • Rockers and switches for the flash, the cameras and which screen holds the controls.
  • A ring dial for the self-timer and a stepper for however many lenses your phone has.
  • Two themes, named for iPhone Duo's own finishes: Star White and Night Sky.

The small things

Simple on purpose.

Shake to develop

Too excited to wait? Give the phone a shake and your snapshot develops immediately.

Handwritten captions

Add a caption along the bottom of your photo to remember the story behind it.

Selfies too

Flip to the front camera, with a wider view for when you need to fit everyone in.

Self-timer

Three or ten seconds. Prop your phone up and step into the picture.

Focus and exposure

Tap to focus, press and hold to lock, then slide to brighten or darken.

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your photos stay on your phone.

Meet the maker

Hi, I'm Ryan.

I've always been a huge fan of Apple products. I waited in line for the original iPhone, and I still remember the feeling of holding it for the first time: new, exciting and fun, like nothing I'd held before. It felt like the future!

Ever since that day I've wanted to build an app of my own. I just never found a reason big enough to start. iPhone Duo is that reason.

It brings that same feeling back, and inspired me to create something for it straight away. Snapshots is my first app, and the first thing I've ever felt compelled to make and share with the world.

I hope Snapshots gives you a little of that feeling too, and a shoebox full of memories worth keeping.

Ryan Fredericks
