Privacy Policy

Snapshots: Instant Film Camera · Effective October 22, 2026

In short: Snapshots doesn't collect, store or share any of your information. Your photos stay on your iPhone.

Information we collect

None. Snapshots has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. The app doesn't send your photos, captions or any other information to us or to anyone else.

Camera

Snapshots asks to use your camera so it can take pictures. The camera is used only while you're using the app, and what it sees is processed on your iPhone.

Your photos and captions

The prints you keep, and their captions, are stored only inside the app on your iPhone. If you delete the app, they're deleted with it.

Photos library

Snapshots asks to add to your Photos library only when you choose to save a print there. It adds the prints you choose and doesn't read your existing photos.

Sharing

When you share a print, you choose where it goes using the share sheet on your iPhone. Snapshots doesn't see or keep a record of what you share.

Settings

Your preferences, such as the theme you choose, are stored on your iPhone so the app remembers them.

Children

Snapshots is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

This website

This website counts visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies, collects no personal information and doesn't follow you to other sites. It records the page opened, the site that linked to it, and the country, browser and screen size, so I can see how many people visit.

This website also uses the X (Twitter) pixel, so I can see how well ads for Snapshots on X work. When you open a page here, or tap a link to the App Store, your browser sends that to X, along with information X uses to connect it to an ad you saw, such as a cookie, your IP address and your browser. X handles this under its own privacy policy: https://x.com/privacy. You can limit it in your X account's privacy settings or by blocking third-party cookies in your browser.

All of this applies to the website only. The app has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and sends nothing to X or anyone else.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
