Questions

How do I take a snapshot?

Press the big round shutter. A printer appears and feeds your photo out. Once it's all the way out it develops over a few seconds. Tap Save Snapshot to keep it, or Discard to throw it away.

Can I make it develop faster?

Yes, give your phone a shake while the photo is developing and it finishes at once.

How do I add a caption?

While the print is on screen, tap "Write a caption" across the bottom of the photo and type. The caption is saved with the print.

How do I change the film style?

Tap the dial beside the shutter. It clicks through Classic, Gold, Coast, Sepia and Mono, and you see each style live in the viewfinder.

How do I focus, or make the picture brighter or darker?

Tap the viewfinder to focus there. Press and hold to lock focus and exposure, then slide your finger up or down to brighten or darken.

Where are my prints, and how do I save them to Photos?

Tap the little photo window beside the shutter to open your gallery. Choose Select, pick prints, then use Save to add them to your Photos library or Share to send them. You can also turn your phone on its side to see your prints laid out like photos on a table.

How do I delete a print?

In the gallery, press and hold a print and choose Delete, or choose Select, pick prints and tap Delete. Deleted prints can't be recovered.

Why is the viewfinder black?

Snapshots needs permission to use the camera. Open the Settings app, scroll to Snapshots and turn on Camera. If another app is using the camera, close it and reopen Snapshots.

Saving to Photos doesn't work.

Open the Settings app, go to Snapshots > Photos and choose "Add Photos Only" or "Full Access".

How does it work on iPhone Duo?

Closed, it works as on any iPhone, on the front display. Open in Landscape, the viewfinder is on one screen and the camera controls on the other; a switch on the controls swaps the sides. Open in Portrait, the viewfinder is above the controls, and pressing the shutter turns the whole display into the printer.

In the Seated pose (half open), take your photo and the print waits part-way out with Unfold Duo to print. Unfold your Duo flat and the print feeds out across the whole display. If you close it instead, the print finishes on the front display.

What is the clock that appears when my phone is on its side?

That's nightstand mode. When your iPhone is charging and turned on its side, Snapshots shows a clock with your prints shuffling past beside it. Tap a print to open it, or tap the gear to change the theme. Unplugged, turning your phone on its side shows your prints in a row instead. On iPhone Duo, the clock appears when you stand it up like a tent.

How do I switch between the light and dark look?

Tap the gear button and choose a theme, or Match Phone to follow your iPhone's setting.
